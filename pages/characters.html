THE WARRENS

ED WARREN

Demonologist & Paranormal Investigator

Self-taught demonologist and one of the world's leading experts on the occult. Co-founder of the New England Society for Psychical Research.

APPEARS IN: All Conjuring Films

LORRAINE WARREN

Clairvoyant & Trance Medium

Gifted clairvoyant and medium who works alongside her husband Ed. Has the ability to see and communicate with spirits.

APPEARS IN: All Conjuring Films

JUDY WARREN

Daughter of Ed & Lorraine Warren

Only child of the Warrens who has inherited some of her mother's psychic abilities.

APPEARS IN: Annabelle Comes Home, The Conjuring 3, The Conjuring 4

DEMONIC ENTITIES

ANNABELLE

Demonic Entity

A malevolent demonic entity that has attached itself to a Raggedy Ann doll, causing terror for decades.

APPEARS IN: Annabelle Trilogy

VALAK

The Demon Nun

A powerful demon that manifests as a horrifying nun, appearing in The Conjuring 2 and The Nun series.

APPEARS IN: The Conjuring 2, The Nun, The Nun II

BATHSHEBA SHERMAN

Witch Spirit

A suspected witch from the 19th century who cursed the Perron farmhouse in Harrisville, Rhode Island.

APPEARS IN: The Conjuring

THE CONJURING SERIES

01

THE CONJURING

2013 | Directed by James Wan

LOCATION: Harrisville, Rhode Island

YEAR SET: 1971

FOCUS: Perron Family Haunting

COMPLETE STORY

In 1971, Roger and Carolyn Perron move into a dilapidated farmhouse in Harrisville, Rhode Island with their five daughters. Almost immediately, paranormal phenomena begin occurring - strange noises, moving objects, and apparitions.

After experiencing increasingly violent activity, Carolyn reaches out to renowned paranormal investigators Ed and Lorraine Warren. The Warrens discover that the house is haunted by the spirit of Bathsheba Sherman, a suspected witch who cursed the land and anyone who tried to take it from her.

The entity grows stronger, attempting to possess members of the family. In a climactic séance, Bathsheba possesses Carolyn, leading to a desperate battle. The Warrens manage to drive the entity away through prayer and ritual, but not before warning that dark forces remain attached to some objects from the house.

CONNECTIONS

Introduces Ed and Lorraine Warren. The haunted music box and other artifacts later appear in the Warren Occult Museum and Annabelle Comes Home.

02

THE CONJURING 2

2016 | Directed by James Wan

LOCATION: Enfield, London, England

YEAR SET: 1977

FOCUS: Enfield Poltergeist

COMPLETE STORY

In 1977, the Hodgson family in Enfield, London begins experiencing poltergeist activity. Single mother Peggy Hodgson and her four children become terrorized as furniture moves, knocking sounds occur, and 11-year-old Janet becomes the primary target.

Ed and Lorraine Warren are initially reluctant due to Lorraine's fears about their previous case, but are convinced to investigate. They discover the presence of Bill Wilkins, an elderly man who died in the house, but soon realize a much darker entity is manipulating the situation.

The demonic nun Valak emerges as the true antagonist, attempting to use Janet as a portal. Through faith and determination, the Warrens confront Valak, eventually banishing the entity. However, Valak would return in future encounters.

CONNECTIONS

Introduces Valak (The Nun). Explains the origin of this demonic entity and connects to the Romanian monastery events.

ANNABELLE

THE RAGGEDY ANN DOLL

HOME / ANNABELLE

"I am not a toy. I am Annabelle." - A demonic entity of great power attached to a Raggedy Ann doll.

POSITIVELY DO NOT OPEN

CASE HISTORY

ORIGIN: Raggedy Ann doll, purchased as a gift for nursing student Donna in 1970

PHENOMENA: Notes written on parchment, doll changing positions, physical attacks

INVESTIGATION: Ed and Lorraine Warren contacted after medium session revealed demonic presence

CONCLUSION: Not the spirit of a child, but an inhuman demonic entity manipulating the doll

CURRENT LOCATION: Warren Occult Museum, Monroe, Connecticut - locked in blessed glass case

MOVIE APPEARANCES

ANNABELLE (2014) - Origins of the doll
ANNABELLE: CREATION (2017) - Dollmaker's tragic story
ANNABELLE COMES HOME (2019) - Back in the Warren Museum

WARNING

The Warrens have stated that Annabelle is "demonically possessed" and should never be provoked. Visitors to the museum are warned to maintain distance from her case.

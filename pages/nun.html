HOME / THE NUN

In 1952 Romania, at the Cârța Monastery, evil has been unleashed. The Vatican sends Father Burke and Sister Irene to investigate, confronting the demonic entity Valak.

THE STORY

THE NUN (2018)

When a young nun commits suicide at the Cârța Monastery in Romania, the Vatican dispatches Father Burke, a priest with a haunted past, and Sister Irene, a novitiate with supernatural visions, to investigate. They uncover the monastery's dark secret - the demonic entity Valak, which was accidentally released centuries ago and now seeks to spread its evil.

THE NUN II (2023)

Four years after the events at the monastery, Sister Irene finds herself once again face to face with Valak. This time, the demonic nun has followed her to a boarding school in France, where more innocent souls are at risk.

VALAK
